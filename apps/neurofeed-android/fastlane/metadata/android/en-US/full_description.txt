NeuroFeed is an open-source neurofeedback trainer for Muse EEG headbands.

* Connects directly to the headband over Bluetooth LE — no cloud, no account.
* Live raw EEG, band power, signal quality and movement views.
* Guided calibration and feedback training sessions with audio/visual feedback.
* Session history with PDF / data export via the system file picker.
* Built-in simulator to try the app without a headband.

All data stays in app-private storage on your phone unless you export it.
Network streaming and the LAN bridge are off by default.
